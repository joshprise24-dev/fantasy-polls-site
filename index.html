HERE TO HELP

Support

Something isn’t working?

Email your username, iPhone model, app version, and what happened. A screenshot and the steps that led to the issue help us investigate.

Remove private information from screenshots. Never send your password, verification codes, or payment details.

Scores and graded picks

Player scores may arrive late or change after a correction. If a result looks wrong, include the poll’s players, week, scoring type, and the score you saw.

FLO measures your ranked-pick results. It has no cash value.

Your profile and account

Tap your profile photo to change it. Username changes are limited to once every 30 days. Manage notification preferences and blocked users in Settings.

Notifications currently appear in the app’s inbox; phone push notifications are not available.

Leaving Fantasy Polls?

You can permanently delete your account from Settings. Shared polls remain so other players keep their picks and results.

KEEP IT ABOUT FOOTBALL

Community rules

Debate the pick. Respect the person. These rules apply to usernames, profile pictures, polls, discussions, comments, and reactions.

  • No harassment, hate, threats, sexual content, or exploitation.
  • No impersonation, scams, spam, or sharing someone’s private information.
  • Only upload pictures and content you have the right to share.

Report a concern

Use the safety menu on a post, comment, or profile to report content or block a user. Manage your blocks in Settings → Safety & blocked users. Blocking does not erase existing picks or public rankings.

For safety questions or to appeal a moderation decision, email fantasypollsapp@gmail.com. Support is not an emergency service.
