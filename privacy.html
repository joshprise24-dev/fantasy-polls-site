Privacy policy

Effective October 27, 2026 · Version 2026-10-27.1

Fantasy Polls is operated by Josh Prise. This policy covers the Fantasy Polls app and this support website.

For privacy questions or requests, email fantasypollsapp@gmail.com.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address, account identifier, username, authentication information, and any signup declarations submitted with your account. Password authentication is handled by Supabase.
  • Profile and activity: an optional profile photo, polls, votes and picks, scores, FLO ratings, badges, follows, discussions, comments, reactions, and in-app notifications and preferences.
  • Safety and support: blocks, reports, reported-content snapshots, moderation decisions, and information you send to our support email.
  • Technical information: our hosting and service providers process request information such as IP addresses, device/browser information, timestamps, authentication events, and errors to deliver and protect the service.

Photo access is used when you choose a profile picture. We upload the picture you select; we do not upload your entire photo library. The app keeps a sign-in session on your device so you can stay signed in.

How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts; display profiles and community content; record and grade picks; calculate rankings and badges; provide in-app updates; investigate reports and prevent abuse; respond to support requests; and diagnose service problems. We also use information when necessary to meet legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising. The current app has no advertising or cross-app tracking SDK. This support website uses no analytics scripts, advertising cookies, or contact forms; its hosting provider still processes ordinary web requests.

What other people can see

Your username, profile photo, follows, profile statistics, and content you post can be visible to other app users. Poll results include voting information according to the app’s display rules. Your email address is not displayed on your public profile. Blocks, private reports, and account preferences are not public profile fields.

Profile photos are stored at publicly accessible URLs. Anyone with a photo URL may view it. Do not upload sensitive information. Blocking limits interactions and visibility within the app; it does not erase rankings, existing picks, or copies others have already made.

Service providers and sharing

We use Supabase for accounts, database storage, profile-photo storage, and backend processing; Expo/EAS and Apple for app building and distribution; GitHub Pages for this website; and Google’s Gmail service for support correspondence. These services process information needed for their roles. Our sports-data integration, including Tank01, supplies player and game information to the backend; we do not send it your email, profile photo, or discussion content to fetch scores.

Authorized operators can access information needed for support, security, and moderation. We may disclose information when required by law or reasonably necessary to address fraud, threats, or the safety and rights of users. Service providers may process information in the United States and other countries where they operate.

Retention and account deletion

We retain account and activity records while your account exists to operate profiles, picks, rankings, and the community. You can delete your account through Profile → Settings → Delete account. See the full deletion instructions.

Deletion removes your authentication account, profile photo, profile, and account-owned records such as votes, follows, comments, and discussions. Replies inside discussions you created are also removed. Shared polls remain without your creator link so other players keep their picks and results.

Separate records may remain. Private moderation reports can include reported content and author identifiers. They are retained separately for investigating reports, addressing repeated abuse, reviewing appeals, and handling legal obligations. Account deletion does not automatically remove these records. Resolved reports become eligible for deletion 180 days after review; moderation action history becomes eligible after 365 days. A daily cleanup removes eligible records in batches. Open cases, records associated with active account or photo restrictions, and records subject to a documented, time-limited safety or legal hold are kept longer. Related held records can also preserve a report or action. Active restrictions and the minimal state needed to keep content hidden remain in effect; inactive account-restriction metadata becomes eligible for deletion after 365 days. Cleanup logs contain only run times and deletion counts and are kept for 90 days. Backlogs or service interruptions can delay cleanup. You may request a review of retained information by emailing support; we assess whether an ongoing safety or legal need requires retaining it.

Support correspondence is kept separately to handle requests and related follow-up; it is not automatically erased by in-app deletion. Provider logs and any backups are governed by the provider’s configured retention and recovery cycles, so account deletion does not immediately remove every historical copy. We do not promise a single deletion deadline covering all of these systems. Contact us about a specific request, including support correspondence.

Your choices and requests

You can change your photo from your profile, change your username subject to the 30-day limit, manage notification preferences and blocked users in Settings, and delete your account. You may email us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information. We may need to verify ownership before acting; never send your password or verification codes.

Depending on where you live and which laws apply, you may have additional privacy rights. We will assess requests under applicable law. If you disagree with how a request was handled, contact the same address and ask for a review.

Age eligibility

Fantasy Polls is intended for people age 13 and older. Users under 18 must have a parent or guardian’s permission. The app is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, email support so we can investigate and remove the account and associated personal information as appropriate.

A signup age declaration is self-reported; it is not identity verification. We do not require a government ID or an exact date of birth in the signup declaration.

Security and policy updates

We use access controls and service-provider security measures to protect information. No system can guarantee complete security. This policy’s date and version will change when we revise it. Material changes will be communicated through the app or another appropriate notice before they take effect where required.
