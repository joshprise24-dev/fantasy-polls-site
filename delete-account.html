YOUR ACCOUNT

Delete your account

You can permanently delete your account directly in Fantasy Polls.

In the app

  1. Open your Profile, then Settings.
  2. Choose Delete account.
  3. Enter your current password and type DELETE to confirm.
  4. Tap Permanently delete account.

This action cannot be undone. Uninstalling the app does not delete your account.

What is removed?

Your authentication account, profile, profile photo, ratings, votes, follows, comments, and discussions are removed. Replies in discussions you created are also removed.

What can remain?

Shared polls remain without your creator link so other players keep their picks and results. Private moderation reports can retain snapshots of reported content and identifiers for safety review. Copies in service-provider backups, logs, and support correspondence may remain separately. Deletion does not remove screenshots or copies other people have made.

Can’t access your account?

Email fantasypollsapp@gmail.com with the subject “Account deletion,” preferably from the email associated with your account. Include your username. We may need to verify account ownership before acting.

Never send your password or verification codes.
